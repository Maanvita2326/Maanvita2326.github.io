Week 1 Tasks

Chart 1 - UK Beach Litter by Brand

Coca-Cola takes the gold medal nobody wanted, with roughly 16% of the branded litter found on UK beaches. PepsiCo and AB InBev are on the second and third position respectively (winning the silver and the bronze medals). The sandcastle competition is clearly not the main event.

Chart 2 - Food CO2 Greenhouse Gas Emission

Beef is the undisputed heavyweight, producing approximately 60 kg of CO2 per kg of food, mostly from landuse and farming. Meanwhile, peas sit at the bottom of the chart, quietly saving the planet.

Chart 3 - Price Variation of a Pub Meal in UK

In the early 90s, a pub meal cost a few quid and now the priciest pubs are well into double digits. The shaded bands show everyone from the bargain pubs to the fancy ones and they have all gone up together.
